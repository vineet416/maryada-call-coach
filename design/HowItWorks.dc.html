A compliance coach that listens while the agent talks

Maryada listens to a recovery call, checks every agent sentence against RBI-style conduct rules, and whispers a short correction to the agent before the next sentence. The borrower never hears it.

From voice to coaching in about three seconds
1. Call audio
The agent and borrower speak. Audio streams in turn by turn.
2. Speech to text
Gnani Prisma turns Indian-language speech, including Hindi and English mixed together, into a live transcript.
3. Rule check
A fast phrase check flags in about 3 s. A deep language check confirms in about 5 s.
4. Risk score
Each finding adds to a 0 to 100 meter. Clean turns bring it down.
5. Coach voice
A short spoken nudge plays in the agent's headset at the next pause.
How to read the transcript

तारंग Finserv से · needs review
आपके personal loan की 2 EMI · context
स्मिता जी · good practice
Tap any underlined phrase to see the rule, why it was flagged, and a better way to say it. Colour is never the only signal: each style also has its own underline.
What the coach does and does not do
✓Speaks only to the agent, in a calm one-line nudge.
✓Names the rule and offers a safer way to continue.
✕Does not give legal advice or decide who is at fault.
✕Does not store your keys, and the demo uses synthetic calls only.
